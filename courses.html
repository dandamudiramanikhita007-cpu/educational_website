Courses Offered

Choose from our industry-oriented academic programs designed to build successful careers.

Tirumala Institutions offers undergraduate, postgraduate and diploma programs that combine classroom learning with practical experience. Our curriculum is regularly updated to meet industry standards and help students develop technical, analytical and communication skills.

B.Tech Computer Science Engineering

Learn programming, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Web Development, Cloud Computing and Cyber Security through practical laboratory sessions and projects.

B.Tech Electronics & Communication Engineering

Study digital electronics, embedded systems, communication technologies, VLSI design and modern electronic applications.

B.Tech Electrical & Electronics Engineering

Gain knowledge in electrical machines, power systems, renewable energy technologies and industrial automation.

B.Tech Mechanical Engineering

Learn manufacturing technology, machine design, robotics, automation and industrial engineering.

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Develop leadership, finance, marketing, human resource management and entrepreneurship skills for successful business careers.

Diploma Programs

Career-oriented diploma programs that provide practical knowledge and excellent employment opportunities.
